Anchovy is what happens when you take the soul of Vietnamese home cooking and run it through a modern share-plate lens. Chef Thi Le's cooking is precise, punchy, and deeply personal.

The **chả lụa toast** has become a signature for good reason — a Vietnamese take on toast with housemade pork sausage that's both familiar and surprising. The **pho broth dumplings** are a genuine wow moment: all the comfort of a bowl of pho in a single bite.

The natural wine list is genuinely thoughtful and the staff know it well. The room on Bridge Rd is casual and neighbourhood-y which makes the quality of the food even more of a pleasant surprise.

**Verdict:** One of Richmond's best, and proof that "modern Australian" is better when it's rooted in something specific.
