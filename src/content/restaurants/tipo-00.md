Hands down my favourite Italian in the city. Tipo 00 does one thing and does it exceptionally well — fresh pasta. The room is small and buzzy, bookings are essential, and the pasta is made in-house daily.

The **cacio e pepe** is textbook: silky, peppery, perfectly al dente. The **tajarin al burro** sounds simple (egg pasta, butter, parmesan) but it's the kind of dish that makes you rethink what simple cooking can be.

Service is warm and knowledgeable without being stuffy. Wine list leans Italian and is well-priced for the quality. Save room for the tiramisu — it's not on every menu rotation but when it is, get it.

**Verdict:** Worth every penny and worth the booking scramble.
