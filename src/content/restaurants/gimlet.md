Gimlet is the rare fine-dining experience that feels genuinely fun. Andrew McConnell's team have nailed the balance between serious cooking and an atmosphere that doesn't take itself too seriously — the room hums, the martinis are cold, and the rotisserie chicken is one of the best things I've eaten all year.

Yes, the **rotisserie chicken**. It sounds pedestrian for a restaurant at this level but it's a masterclass in what technique and quality produce can do to a simple idea. Crisp skin, impossibly juicy, with a jus that belongs in a museum.

The bar program is exceptional. Get a Gimlet martini while you wait — it's the cleanest, coldest, most perfectly balanced martini in Melbourne.

**Verdict:** Expensive, worth it, and one of the few places that actually justifies the hype.
