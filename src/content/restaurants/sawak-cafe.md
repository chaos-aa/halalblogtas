Sawak Cafe is one of those places Hobart should be proud of. Tucked on Collins Street, it's been quietly serving some of the most authentic Malaysian street food in Tasmania since 2010, cooking the food that owner and chef Zye Chong grew up eating in Sarawak.

The laksa is the headline act and deservedly so. It won Tassie's laksa category in the Delicious 100 for 2023. A deep coconut milk broth, chilli oil floating on top, vermicelli, tofu puffs, a hard-boiled egg, beansprouts and coriander, with your choice of protein. The kind of bowl that makes a cold Hobart day bearable. The har mee prawn soup is equally serious. A rich, sweet prawn broth with real depth. Portions are generous and prices are genuinely reasonable for the quality.

My personal go-to order would of course be the Tomyum Fried Rice with Chicken and an extra Fried Egg on top. The taste reminds of my own time living in Malaysia and the flavours all bring together this wonderful dish that gives you taste of Malaysia and its beautiful cuisine.

Address: 131 Collins St, Hobart TAS 7000
